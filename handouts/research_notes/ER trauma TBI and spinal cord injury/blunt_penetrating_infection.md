### Batch 2 - CDC tetanus page + EAST 2022-2026 PubMed sweep + open fracture literature 2021-2026 [verified = abstract/page read]
- CDC "Clinical Guidance for Wound Management to Prevent Tetanus" (cdc.gov/tetanus/hcp/clinical-guidance/index.html; published May 15, 2024; updated/reviewed Sept 8, 2026). Read via WebFetch summary: [verified-page, paraphrased by fetch tool]
  - Unknown history, unvaccinated, or incomplete primary series -> vaccinate for ALL wounds.
  - Complete primary series, clean and minor wound -> vaccinate if last dose >=10 years ago.
  - Complete primary series, dirty or major wound -> vaccinate if last dose >=5 years ago.
  - Last dose <5 years ago and primary series complete -> no vaccine regardless of wound.
  - Dirty/major wound examples: penetrating or puncture wounds; wounds with dirt, soil, feces, or saliva (animal/human bites); devitalized tissue (burns, compound fractures, crush injuries, frostbite, necrotic/gangrenous wounds).
  - TIG: clean minor wounds -> TIG "never indicated"; dirty/major wounds -> TIG for unknown history, never vaccinated, incomplete primary series, HIV infection, or severe immunodeficiency. Dose 250 international units IM. CDC does not stockpile TIG; commercial purchase.
  - "Topical or systemic antibiotics are not recommended to prevent tetanus."
  - Page uses "complete primary series" wording (older MMWR table wording is ">=3 doses") [the >=3-dose wording is recalled from Liang 2018 Table, not re-read].
