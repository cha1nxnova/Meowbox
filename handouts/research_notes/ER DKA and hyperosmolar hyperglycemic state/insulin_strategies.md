# Insulin strategies for adult DKA and HHS in the US emergency department

**Scope:** Adults (non-pregnant) with diabetic ketoacidosis (DKA) or hyperglycemic hyperosmolar state (HHS) in the US emergency department (ED), from arrival through stabilization, handoff or ED discharge. Covers IV regular insulin infusion, subcutaneous (SC) rapid-acting analog protocols, early basal insulin, dextrose titration, transition to SC insulin, computerized protocols, hypoglycemia/hypokalemia rates, and pharmacy practicalities. Pediatrics: one-line pointer only (covered by another researcher).
